About Sher Wine Shop

A small shop built around one habit: tasting before selling.

Our story

Sher Wine Shop started in 2018 as a Saturday tasting table in a Napa garage. Friends kept asking which bottle to bring to dinner, so we began writing down what we poured and why.

Today we work with 40 family vineyards and ship across the United States, still tasting every wine before it is listed.

Mission

Make good wine easy to choose, with honest notes and fair prices.

Vision

A wine culture where adults enjoy what they drink and know where it came from.

What makes us different

Taste-tested

Nothing is listed until our team has tried it.

Small producers

Family vineyards, mostly under 5,000 cases a year.

Plain language

Notes that say what you will taste, not what a critic scored.
